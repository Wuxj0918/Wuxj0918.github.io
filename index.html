I am currently a third-year Ph.D. student in Information and Communication Engineering at the Beijing Institute of Technology supervised by Prof. Feifeng Liu and Prof. Tao Zeng.

Previously, I received the B.S. degree from Electronic and Information Engineering at JiLin University in 2022.

My research interests include change detection technology based on global navigation satellite system-based SAR interferometry systems, particularly in:

  • GNSS-based InSAR deformation monitoring and inversion
  • Multi-satellite collaborative observation and data fusion for deformation estimation
  • High-frequency deformation monitoring and early warning for infrastructure and geohazards

📧 Please feel free to contact me if you would like to explore potential discussions or collaborations.
